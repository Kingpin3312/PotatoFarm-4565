How it works

It never invents a fact about a property.

It answers from your listings and nothing else. Not the view, not the service charge, not whether the landlord will negotiate — unless that’s on the record. If it doesn’t know, it says so and fetches an agent.

“I’ve got this” silences the assistant on one conversation so an agent can take over a delicate one. “Stop everything” halts every conversation in the brokerage immediately — no delay, no cache.
