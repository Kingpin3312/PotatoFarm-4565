# Hot, warm, cold, dead

The best idea in the mockup, and I want to say why before it gets built
so nobody later decides it is a gimmick and takes it out.

## Why it works

I spent three rounds arguing that "Potato" undercuts a compliance
product — that a brokerage owner handing over their client list and AML
file to something called Potato is a hard sell. **That argument was
right about the name alone and wrong about this.**

A name you have to apologise for is a liability. A name that gives you a
taxonomy is an asset. **Hot Potatoes** is not a joke about the brand, it
is what an agent already calls that lead in their head — and now the
software calls it the same thing.

It also solves the problem the name created. Nobody explains "PotatoFarm"
awkwardly in a meeting if the first screen says *Hot Potatoes 63*, because
by then it is obviously deliberate. **Committing to a strange name is what
makes it stop being strange.**

"Grow your farm. Close more deals." finishes the job. The farming
metaphor was sitting in the name the whole time and I did not see it.

## The four, and what each one means

An agent must be able to sort their day by these, so they cannot be
vibes. Each is a rule.

| | Means | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot** | Ready now | Viewing booked, or an offer discussed, or asked about payment plans |
| **Warm** | Real but not yet | Qualified — budget and timeframe known — and engaged in the last 14 days |
| **Cold** | Gone quiet | Qualified, nothing in 14 days. **Not dead** — this is where money is left |
| **Dead** | Closed | Bought elsewhere, out of market, or asked us to stop |

**Cold is the important one.** Most brokerages treat cold as dead and
delete it from their thinking. A cold lead is a qualified buyer who went
quiet, and the matching engine already knows what they wanted — the day a
listing lands that fits, they are the first message out.

**Dead is not a bin.** Somebody who asked us to stop must never be
messaged again, and that is a legal position as much as a courteous one.
Dead is a permanent instruction, not a low score.

## Not the same as pipeline stage

Temperature is **how the person feels.** Stage is **where the deal is.**
Conflating them is the mistake — a lead can be hot and at first contact,
or cold and under offer while a mortgage drags.

Two axes, and both are needed.

## Colour is never the only signal

Every one of the four passes AA as **text** on both grounds, because a
temperature is read rather than glanced at. And the word is always
present next to the dot — an agent who is colourblind still needs to know
which of their leads is hot, and roughly one in twelve men is.
