# The stats bar and the logos

Two things in the mockup that I am not going to build, and this is the
reasoning rather than a refusal.

## The stats

    25K+     Active Users
    $4.2B+   Property Value Sold
    120K+    Deals Closed
    98%      Client Retention

**None of these are true.** There are no users, no deals, and no
retention to measure. The CRM has never been compiled.

Two turns ago I removed four invented performance figures from this same
homepage — *38s median reply, +31% viewings*, attributed to a "pilot
brokerage" that does not exist. This is the same defect an order of
magnitude larger, and it sits above the fold.

The failure mode is not abstract. **In a market this size, the first
brokerage owner who asks "which twenty-five thousand?" ends the meeting
and tells people.** A UAE brokerage principal who has been told a
supplier inflated their numbers repeats it, and there are perhaps two
hundred firms worth selling to.

## The logos

    EMAAR · DAMAC · SOBHA · BINGHATTI · OMNIYAT

Under the words **"Trusted by leading real estate companies."**

These are the five largest developers in the country and none of them
has heard of us. Beyond being untrue, using a registered trademark to
imply a commercial relationship that does not exist is the kind of thing
their legal teams have people for.

It is also the specific risk you can least afford: **you have a real
business in this market.** EDM works with Tier-1 contractors here. A
letter from Emaar's counsel about a different company you own is not a
software problem.

## What goes there instead

The mockup is right that the space under the hero needs something — a
blank gap there reads as a page that has not loaded.

**Fill it with what is true and specific**, which is more persuasive than
a number nobody can check:

> **Built for how property actually works here.**
> Trakheesi permits. DLD transfer timelines. AML obligations for a
> DNFBP. The things a CRM designed in London does not know about.
>
> `Trakheesi` · `Form F to transfer` · `goAML` · `RERA` · `AED escrow`

Four terms a Dubai brokerage owner recognises instantly and no
international competitor puts on their homepage. It says *we are from
here* — which is the claim Goyzer and Reapit structurally cannot make and
the only advantage that is genuinely yours today.

## When the real numbers exist

Put them up the day they are true, name the brokerage, and quote the
owner. **One named customer with a real figure beats four invented
totals**, and it will be worth more in three months than anything on that
bar is worth now.

The logos go up when you have a signed permission email. Not before.
