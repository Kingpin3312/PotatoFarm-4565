Security

Who can reach your data.

Short answers, and the ones we cannot answer yet are marked as such rather than dressed up.

Three short answers.

01

Separation

Every brokerage's data is isolated by the database itself, not by application code. There is no query path that can reach another firm's leads.

02

Our access

Nobody at Potato can see your data unless you let us in — to a named person, for a stated reason, for a maximum of 72 hours. You see every time we did.

03

Deletion

Ask and it goes, except where UAE anti-money-laundering law requires us to keep a due diligence record. We tell you which, and when it will go.

Every action is recorded and cannot be edited.

The audit log is append-only at the database level — not by policy, by permission. Nothing in the application can alter it, including us.
