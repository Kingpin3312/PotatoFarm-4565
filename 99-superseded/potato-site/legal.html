Contents

  • Cookie policy — what runs, and the consent banner that gates it

Two notes

These need a lawyer, not a template. You are handling personal data belonging to third parties — the leads, not just your customers — and in a market where a good share of buyers are European. That combination is worth a proper review.

Second, the cookie banner has to actually gate the analytics rather than sit on top of them. Kendal loads Google Tag Manager with no visible consent mechanism at all, which is a live exposure for anyone with UK or EU traffic. Easy to get right at build time and awkward to retrofit.
