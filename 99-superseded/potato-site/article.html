Why response time beats lead volume almost every time.

28 July 20263 min readBy [author]

When enquiries stop converting, the instinct is to buy more of them. It rarely works, because the problem usually isn’t how many arrive. It’s how long they sit.

The maths nobody runs

Take the enquiries you received last month, and mark the time each one was first answered. Then mark which ones turned into a viewing. In most brokerages the pattern is obvious within about ten rows.
