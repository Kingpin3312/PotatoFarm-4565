How it works

Three steps, and only one of them involves your team.

01

A lead lands

Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle, your own website, a WhatsApp click on an ad. All of it arrives in one place.

02

Potato replies

Within seconds, in the lead's own language, asking the questions you'd want asked. Budget, timeframe, whether they're buying to live in or to let out.

03

Your agent picks it up
