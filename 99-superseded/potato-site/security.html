A word on what goes on this page

Only what is true and evidenced. Never a certification you are working towards, never a claim you cannot show a document for. This page exists to survive a due diligence question, and a claim that falls over in that meeting costs you the deal and the reputation. If you are not certified yet, say what you actually do instead. That reads as honest, and honest sells better than a badge nobody checks.
