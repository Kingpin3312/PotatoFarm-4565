Security

Who can reach your data.

Short answers, and the ones we cannot answer yet are marked as such rather than dressed up. You are handing over your client list; you are entitled to specifics.

Separation

Enforced by the database, not by our code.

Every brokerage's data is isolated using PostgreSQL row-level security. That means the separation is a property of the database itself rather than something our application remembers to check.

The distinction matters. Application-level separation is one forgotten where clause away from showing you somebody else's leads. With row-level security there is no query path that reaches another firm's data, including one written by mistake.

Our access

We cannot see your data unless you let us in.

There is no support account with a master key. When you need help, you grant access — to a named person, for a stated reason, for a maximum of 72 hours.

You can see every grant that has ever been made and revoke any of them instantly. Anyone on your team can revoke; only an admin can grant. The asymmetry is deliberate.

Every action is recorded and cannot be edited.

The audit log is append-only at the database level — not by policy, by permission. Nothing in the application can alter or delete a row, including us, because the application account does not hold the right to.

If support does something on your behalf it is recorded as support, with the grant and the person named. You will never find a change attributed to one of your managers that was actually made by us.

Deletion

Ask and it goes, with one lawful exception.

A buyer asking to be forgotten is honoured. Messages are erased, personal details are removed, and the record that an erasure happened is kept without keeping the person.

The exception is customer due diligence. UAE anti-money-laundering law requires a brokerage to keep those records for five years, including for a deal that collapsed, and a statutory obligation overrides a deletion request. When that applies we tell you plainly which records are held and the date they will go — rather than quietly erasing them and leaving you unable to answer an inspection.

Compliance

Built for a DNFBP, because that is what you are.

Every brokerage concluding a purchase or sale in the UAE is a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession. That brings obligations with real penalties attached, and almost no CRM in this market mentions them.

Screening

Before onboarding

Customers and beneficial owners checked against the UAE Local Terrorist List and the UN Consolidated List. Nothing is auto-cleared — a possible match goes to your compliance officer with the detail, and a person decides.

Reporting

The cash threshold

A Real Estate Activity Report is triggered at AED 55,000 in cash, single or linked. Linked is the one that gets missed — three payments of twenty thousand across a week aggregate, and we catch that.

Records

Five years

Due diligence records retained for five years even where the transaction never completed, with an audit trail an inspector can read. Reports are prepared here and filed by your compliance officer on goAML — we do not submit on your behalf, and you should be wary of anyone who offers to.

This is a starting point built from Ministry of Economy guidance and the FIU's published requirements. It supports your compliance officer; it does not replace them, and nothing here is legal advice.
