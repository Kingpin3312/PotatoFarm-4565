Fifteen minutes, and we start with your numbers.

Before we show you anything, we look at how quickly your team replies today. That is the whole argument, and it is yours rather than ours.

hello@potatofarm.io
On the call

Four questions, and one of them is uncomfortable.

01

How many enquiries?

Roughly, per month, across every portal. We are looking for whether the volume justifies this at all — below about two hundred a month it usually does not.

02

Where do they come from?

Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle, your own site, referrals. Different sources behave differently and the mix changes what is worth doing first.

03

Who answers at nine at night?

This is the uncomfortable one. Most brokerages answer honestly and the answer is "whoever is awake". Some answer "nobody, and it has never been a problem" — and if that is genuinely true, we will tell you this is not for you.

What happens next

A week of measuring before anything is switched on.

If it goes further, the first week is a baseline. The assistant stays off and we record how quickly enquiries are answered today, broken down by hour.

Almost nobody has seen that chart for their own business. It is usually the moment the conversation stops being about software.

Week 0Baseline, assistant off
Week 1Drafts only, agent approves
Week 2Live out of hours
Weeks 3–4Full, with your numbers

We agree what would make us stop, in advance.

One invented property fact and it stops. Viewings per hundred enquiries below your baseline and it stops. More than four in ten conversations handed to a human and it stops.

Written down before we start, so neither of us gets to reinterpret them later.

Straight answer

Who this is not for.

Fewer than eight agents — the overhead is not worth it. Fewer than two hundred enquiries a month — you can answer those by hand and should. Mostly lettings and property management — that is a different product and we would be a worse version of one you already have.
