The problem

Nobody loses a lead. They lose the race.

Every enquiry you get, three other brokerages got as well. Property Finder, Bayut and Dubizzle send the same buyer to everyone with a matching listing, at the same moment.

Overnight

Not the best one. The one who happened to be awake. That conversation is now running and the other three have not started.

Morning

You reply fourth

To somebody who has already booked a viewing with somebody else. The lead was never lost — it was answered, by a competitor, at 21:04.

This is not a CRM problem. Your pipeline is not leaking. You are being outrun in the first ten minutes, every night, by whoever is holding a phone.

How it works

Three things, then it hands over.

It is not trying to sell anything. It is trying to hold the conversation open until one of your agents can take it seriously.

01

It replies

Within seconds of the enquiry landing, from your number, in the language the buyer wrote in. Arabic or English, without being told which.

02

It qualifies

Budget, buying or renting, timeframe, mortgage or cash. Your questions, asked the way you would ask them — not a form with a chat window round it.

03

It steps back

The moment it matters, your agent takes over with the whole conversation already there. It hands over on purpose, not on failure.

It never invents a fact about a property.

It answers from your listings and nothing else. Not the view, not the service charge, not whether the landlord will negotiate — unless that is on the record. If it does not know, it says so and fetches an agent.

Two controls, and they do different things on purpose. “I’ve got this” silences the assistant on one conversation, so an agent can take over a delicate one without affecting anybody else. “Stop everything” halts every conversation in the brokerage immediately — no delay, no cache, no five minutes of messages still going out.
