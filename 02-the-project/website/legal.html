What we hold, and for how long

Written to be read rather than survived. If anything here is unclear, email us and we will fix the wording rather than explain it.

01

Your data is yours

Leads, conversations and listings belong to your brokerage. You can export all of it at any time, including during a billing dispute.

02

Deletion

Ask and it goes — except customer due diligence records, which UAE anti-money-laundering law requires us to keep for five years. We tell you which records those are and the date they will be removed.

03

If you leave an email

We use it to send the guide you asked for and the next one, and nothing else. We don’t sell it, we don’t pass it on, and one reply saying stop is the end of it. If you’d rather we deleted it entirely, say so and we will.

04

Sub-processors

Meta for WhatsApp delivery, and our hosting and payment providers. We publish the list and tell you before it changes.
