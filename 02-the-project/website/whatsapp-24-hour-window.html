Why your WhatsApp message never arrived

Meta's 24-hour rule, what it actually does, and why the failure is silent — which is the part that costs brokerages deals without anyone noticing.

The rule

A business can send a free-form WhatsApp message only within 24 hours of the customer's last inbound message. Outside that window, only a pre-approved template is permitted.

This applies to every business on the WhatsApp Business Platform. It is not something your provider chose and not something any of them can waive.

Why nobody notices

Here is the part that matters. When you send outside the window, the message does not bounce. The API accepts it. It appears sent in your system. It is simply never delivered.

So an agent follows up on a lead who went quiet three days ago, sees a sent message, and concludes the buyer is ignoring them. The buyer never received anything. Both sides are behaving reasonably and the deal quietly dies.

What to do about it

01

Know where the window is

Track the last inbound message per conversation and show the countdown to the agent before they type, not after they send.

02

Get templates approved early

Meta approval takes time. A follow-up template you need on Tuesday is one you should have submitted a fortnight ago.

03

Answer fast enough that it rarely applies

The window is only a problem for conversations that have gone cold. Replying within minutes means most never do.
