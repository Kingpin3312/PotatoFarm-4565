What being a DNFBP actually means

If your brokerage concludes purchases or sales, UAE anti-money-laundering law already applies to you. Here is the plain-English version of what it requires.

You are already in scope

A Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession is a category that includes real estate brokers concluding transactions. There is no threshold to cross and no registration that starts the clock — the obligation attaches to the activity.

What it requires

Registration on goAML

The FIU's reporting system, plus the EOCN system. Both are prerequisites rather than things you do when you have something to report.

Customer due diligence, on everyone

Identity, source of funds and source of wealth — two different questions, and the guidance is explicit that both are required. Enhanced diligence for non-residents and politically exposed persons.

Sanctions screening before onboarding

Against the UAE Local Terrorist List and the UN Consolidated List, for the customer and for any beneficial owner behind a corporate buyer. Shell companies are the mechanism the whole obligation exists to catch.

Real Estate Activity Reports

Filed when a transaction involves AED 55,000 or more in physical cash, single or linked — and for any virtual asset settlement regardless of amount. Linked is the one that gets missed.

Five-year records

Kept even where the deal collapsed. A right-to-erasure request does not override this; the statutory obligation wins.

A named compliance officer

And a rule most people miss: telling a client that a report has been filed, or is being considered, is tipping off — an offence in its own right, separate from anything the client may have done.

Written from Ministry of Economy guidance and the FIU's published requirements. It is a starting point for a conversation with a compliance adviser, not legal advice, and you should treat anybody who offers it as advice with suspicion.

Screening, due diligence records, the cash threshold and a compliance officer role who can see the reports while an admin cannot — built in rather than left to a spreadsheet.
