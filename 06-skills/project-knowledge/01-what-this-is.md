# What this is

**PotatoFarm.io** — a WhatsApp-native lead qualification CRM for UAE
brokerages. $70 per agent per month, 60 pooled conversations per agent,
35 fils overage.

Christopher Simon, COO of EDM Holdings, Dubai. Mobile-first. Direct,
minimal, high professional standards.

## Where it stands

- **Website** — 10 pages, deployable today, every claim defensible
- **Backend** — 66 models, 21 routers, 22 scheduled jobs. Complete
- **Interface** — 32 screens, **95% of procedures reachable**
- **Brand** — one mark across seven surfaces, enforced by a check
- **16 audit scripts** — all passing

## What is not true yet

- **It has never been compiled.** No `npm install`, no database, no
  migration has ever run. Expect real errors on the first build; that is
  normal for a codebase this size and does not mean the work is wrong.
- **It has never sent a message.** No WhatsApp number, no Meta app, no
  Stripe keys.
- **There is no customer.** This has been the unchanged bottom line
  through every review.
