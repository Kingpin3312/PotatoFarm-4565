# Launch readiness — honest assessment

You asked me to compare. The comparison is not the finding.

## The comparison

| | Old site | v3 (approved design) |
|---|---|---|
| Design | Paper and ink, 12 sections, serif | **Approved. Navy, 7 sections, one family** |
| Pages | 11 | 11 |
| Written words | **3,167** | 1,105 |
| Stub pages | 0 | **6** |
| All audits | Pass | Pass |

**v3 is the better design with a third of the content. The old site is
the older design with all of it.**

## Neither is ready to go live tomorrow

This is not a design problem and it is not fixable overnight by me.

**On v3, six of eleven pages say "More on this page shortly."** Including
Legal, which is a legal requirement, and Customers, which is the second
page a brokerage owner opens.

**On the old site, twenty placeholder markers are still live** — nine of
them on the homepage. The pricing page says **`AED —`**. The customers
page says **"Brokerage name / One line on the problem they had before /
Result pending."**

If the world is genuinely looking tomorrow, it will look at a pricing
page with no price and a customers page listing a brokerage called
"Brokerage name". That is worse than not launching.

## What is actually blocking

The same four things, unchanged since the site was first built. None of
them are mine to fix:

1. **A price.** One number.
2. **Three testimonials with a figure attached.** You have no customers,
   so you have none. This is the honest blocker.
3. **Client logos with written permission.** Same problem.
4. **Hosting region and retention period**, matching the privacy policy.

Two and three cannot be solved by tomorrow. **You do not have a customer
yet.** That is not a criticism, it is the position, and the site has to
tell the truth about it.

## What I would ship instead

**Cut the site to what is true.** Four pages, all complete, no gaps:

- **Home** — the approved design, already written
- **Product** — written
- **Security** — written, and your strongest material
- **Book a call** — written

**Remove Pricing, Customers, Integrations, Blog, About, Legal-as-stub.**

A four-page site with nothing missing reads as focused. An eleven-page
site with six "coming soon" pages and a customers page full of
placeholders reads as a company that is not ready — which is the exact
impression you cannot afford on day one.

Nobody has ever visited a site and thought "only four pages, they must be
small". They think it when they find the empty ones.

**Pricing:** replace with a line on the home page — "Per agent, per
month. Book a call and we'll talk about it." That is a normal position
for a product at this stage and it costs you nothing.

**Legal:** this one you do need. It is short, it is a genuine
requirement, and a lawyer or a template gets it done today.

## My recommendation, plainly

**Launch the four-page version of v3 tomorrow.** It is the approved
design, everything on it is true, and nothing on it is waiting.

Add Pricing the day you set a number. Add Customers the day you have
one, with a real name and a real figure. That page will be worth ten
times more then than a placeholder version is now.

The thing that would be hardest to recover from is not launching small.
It is launching with `AED —` on a page the world is looking at.
