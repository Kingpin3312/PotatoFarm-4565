# S.MPLE — what it is, and what to actually do about it

## You cannot buy it, and that is the whole story

S.MPLE is **free to SERHANT agents and available only to SERHANT
agents**. Inman reported the rollout: agents pay nothing beyond their
normal commission split.

It is not a SaaS product. It is a **recruiting weapon**. One of their own
testimonials says it plainly — *"It's why we joined SERHANT."*

**SERHANT is not competing with us for customers. They are competing
with brokerages for agents.** That is a different fight, and treating it
as the same one leads to building the wrong thing.

## What it does

An agent speaks or texts a request. Behind it, the request is mapped to
one of **17 "Recipes"** — CMA, listing presentation, newsletter, social
post, content calendar, transaction deadlines. Software does the work.
**Then a human Advisor runs quality control before it goes back.**

The results are worth respecting rather than dismissing:

| | |
|---|---|
| NPS | 97 |
| Repeat use within 5 days | 95% |
| 1,400 requests | ~5,000 hours saved |

That is roughly **3.5 hours saved per request**. Agents clearly want
this.

## Three reasons it does not port to Dubai

1. **MLS-bound.** Every recipe that matters — CMA above all — assumes
   MLS comparables. **Dubai has no MLS.** There is DLD transaction data,
   portal listings, and whatever the brokerage itself has done.
2. **US-licensed.** SERHANT is a broker in 16 states and DC.
3. **Staffed.** The advisor layer is people. It scales by hiring.

## What to take, and what to leave

### Take: voice → deliverable

**This is genuinely the right interaction** and we do not have it. An
agent between viewings cannot type. "Met someone new, drop a voice note,
we handle the CRM update and the follow-up" is correct, and it is the
single best idea either competitor has had.

### Leave: the human advisor

**Their quality control is a human. That is their constraint, not their
moat.**

- It caps throughput at how many advisors you employ
- It adds hours of latency — their own testimonial celebrates a request
  submitted at one showing arriving by the third
- It puts a services gross margin under a software business

We should do the same job with **software plus an honest confidence
gate** — produce it in seconds when the data supports it, and say so
plainly when it does not, rather than guessing.

## Where we can be better, specifically

**We have the data a deliverable needs and they have to go fetch it.**

A CMA needs comparables. A listing presentation needs viewing history
and offer activity. A vendor report needs what actually happened this
week. PotatoFarm already holds listings, viewings, offers, vendors and
deals for that brokerage.

Their advisor opens the MLS. Ours reads the brokerage's own book.

## The pattern worth noticing

Kendal Co-Work sells to the agent. S.MPLE serves the agent. **Two
competitors have independently bet that the agent is the buyer.**

That is evidence, not proof. It does not mean the brokerage-side thesis
is wrong — compliance, the vendor side and the transaction record are
firm obligations that no agent tool can hold. But it does mean the agent
has to *want* to open this product, and until now we have designed for
the owner who pays rather than the agent who uses it.

**Voice → deliverable is how the agent gets a reason to open it.**

## What is being built

1. **Voice request pipeline** — speak a request, it is classified,
   executed and returned in seconds
2. **Dubai comparables report** — the CMA equivalent, grounded in DLD
   transactions and the brokerage's own history, because there is no MLS
3. **A confidence gate** — thin evidence produces a stated caveat or a
   refusal, never a confident number

**The rule that governs all three: never invent a comparable.** A CMA an
agent shows a seller is a number they will be held to. Being wrong there
costs the instruction, and it is the one place where being fast is worth
nothing.
