# The mark

A potato, beside the wordmark. Orange body, darker speckles, on cream —
and on a leather plate for the app icon, so it stands out on a home
screen full of white ones.

## What went wrong first

I designed a smooth ovoid with three dots in a row, on the theory that a
potato's eyes and a typing indicator are the same three marks. It was a
neat idea and it was the wrong answer: it read as a speech bubble, and
the brief was a potato.

**The lesson is not subtle.** A concept that needs explaining has already
failed. A logo does not get a paragraph next to it.

## What the drawing needed

**Lumps.** The first silhouette was smooth, which is what made it a
bubble. Real potatoes are knobbly — uneven radii, one shoulder fatter
than the other, a flatter underside.

**Uneven speckles.** Six identical dots read as a ladybird. Nine of
varying size, scattered rather than arranged, read as a potato.

**Room to breathe.** Inset to 80% inside the app icon plate. iOS and
Android crop to their own masks, and a mark touching the edge loses its
shoulders.

## Two colourways, two jobs

**In the product: orange potato, cream ground.** It sits in a nav and a
sidebar that are already cream, and it needs to be the warm thing on a
quiet page.

**As an app icon: light potato, leather plate.** The opposite, and
deliberately. A home screen is a wall of white and near-white tiles —
Messages, Photos, Notes, Settings. A cream icon disappears into it. The
plate measures 11.58:1 against a white tile, which is not a readability
figure, it is a separation one.

Same drawing, inverted. Not two marks.

## The rules

- **Two colourways.** Orange on cream, cream on leather. Ink on cream for
  one-colour print. No gradients.
- **Never outline it.** Solid fill only — the speckles become holes in a
  stroke and it falls apart small.
- **Clear space is the mark's own width** on every side.
- **Below 24px the speckles go and the silhouette carries it.** That is
  intended: the shape is distinctive enough on its own, and speckles at
  16px are a smear.
- **The speckles are fixed.** Do not re-scatter them. Their unevenness is
  what makes the shape read as a potato rather than a bean.
